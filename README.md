# AI tool for policy transparency in ballots and voting
Voters frequently face ballot measures that are confusing, technical, or time-consuming to research. To address this, we propose a simple AI tool using agentic AI and Retrieval Augmentation (RAG) to help simplify ballot language and increases policy transparency, helping people understand what they are voting on.
